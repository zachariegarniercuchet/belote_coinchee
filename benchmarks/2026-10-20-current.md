# Current bots — simulation benchmark, 20 October 2026

Historical snapshot of the frozen revision below. It PRECEDES the subsequent
side-strength opening requirement, first-side-ace-in-base convention, immediate
extra-ace entry credits and permission to switch suits. These are not win rates
for the newly amended Brooklyn v2.

The latest Brooklyn v2 corrections improve contract success on the reused
development sample, but v1 remains substantially stronger in these matchups.
V2's average bid is now close to v1's; its chosen contracts are still less
reliable. Both Brooklyn versions already draw trump at every measured choice
opportunity. That is not evidence that drawing more would improve de Belote.

## Method and checks

- Frozen source: `edbd52e639faa9132ce7036fbc4b6014acacf718`.
- 400 full games: Brooklyn v1/v2 against de Belote v2/v3, 100 per matchup.
- Web `GameSession` / `NetworkGame`, target 1000, default bot search settings.
  Display sleeps/broadcasts disabled; live server untouched.
- Seeds 30000–30049, each replayed with Brooklyn on teams 0 and 1; dealer
  `seed % 4`, per-seat bot RNG `seed * 17 + seat`. Runtime about 9.3 minutes.
- All games completed; no dropped/error games. Seed swaps, outcome totals,
  taking totals and v1's prior 53/50-win baselines were checked.
- Eight short instrumented/uninstrumented game replays matched bids and winners
  before the main run. Lead measurement observes hidden hands only after each
  actual choice; those hands never reach the decision code.
- Win intervals: approximate 95% percentile bootstrap, 4,000 resamples of whole
  seed pairs. Correlated games/deals are not treated as independent trials.
  These are pointwise exploratory intervals, not simultaneous ranking tests.
- No tuning after results. No bot code or live games changed for this study.

Same seeds as the earlier study: useful for development comparisons, NOT an
independent holdout. Different bidding can change game lengths and subsequent
hands; seed matching does not fix every later deal or contract. No formal
hypothesis-test p-values or global Elo ratings are claimed.

## Trump-drawing behaviour

An opportunity is an ACTUAL attacking lead decision with both a legal trump
and a side-suit card, while at least one opponent actually holds trump.
Claims and automatic last tricks are excluded: the bots made no choice there.
Both attacking partners count. Jack-leading has the additional condition
that the player holds the trump jack. Counts below pool the two opponent
matchups for each bot; their contract/hand mixes differ.

| Bot | Trump leads / opportunities | Rate | Jack led / jack held opportunities |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Brooklyn v1 | 1075/1075 | 100% | 198/198 (100%) |
| Brooklyn v2 | 1041/1041 | 100% | 232/232 (100%) |
| de Belote v2 | 602/1588 | 37.9% | 219/646 (33.9%) |
| de Belote v3 | 673/1653 | 40.7% | 249/705 (35.3%) |

De Belote v2/v3 led a side-suit ace in 257/1588 (16.2%) and 239/1653 (14.5%)
of those opportunities. The remaining non-trump leads can be other side cards.
These are behavioural frequencies, NOT labels of good/bad play. A causal
comparison of trump-drawing rules needs fixed hands/contracts and an A/B
card-play policy test; this tournament does not supply that comparison.

## Exploratory opening diagnostic for Brooklyn v2

This describes the ORIGINAL opener's hand in the final team-suit episode,
not the last partner to bid. Results pool both opponents (correlated samples).

| Opening trump holding | Contracts made | Rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Exactly 3, nine without jack | 51/121 | 42.1% |
| Exactly 3, jack without nine | 105/159 | 66.0% |
| Exactly 3, both jack and nine | 48/72 | 66.7% |
| At least 4 trumps, any permitted honours | 170/225 | 75.6% |

Three-trump nine-only openings account for 70 of v2's 203 failed contracts,
although they are only 121 of 577 contracts. This is a strong investigation
target, not proof that every nine-only opening should be forbidden: side-suit
strength, partner support, opponents and eventual bids also differ. Lowering
all bid amounts again would not directly address this selection issue.

## Reproduce and inspect

See [README.md](README.md) and [results-2026-10-20.json](results-2026-10-20.json).
The summary includes all eight side-specific rows and the 400 game outcomes
needed to reproduce win intervals. Use `--output` to retain full per-game deal
records, including the metrics from which the summary is calculated.

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B benchmarks/bots.py --pairs 50 --workers 4 --output results.json
```
