# Brooklyn v4 — versioned trump-follow clarification, 26 October 2026

V4 wins 49/100 games against de Belote v2 and 43/100 against v3, versus
46/100 in each newly rerun v3 control. Overall both win 92/200 in this balanced
sample. Neither paired v4-minus-v3 interval excludes zero; no overall upgrade
is established on these development seeds.

## Version mapping and exact scope

- Frozen runtime source: `09e0bf99ec74bea5e3e161e1c073578be352f5ce`.
- V4 inherits v3's recoverable-card bidding, partnership credits, public
  first-bidder memory and first-trump/partner-return lead policy.
- ONLY while FOLLOWING an existing trump lead and attacking, v4 takes with
  the strongest legal winner. In cutting/overcutting a side suit, it retains
  the smallest sufficient winner in attack. Compulsory overtrump legality
  still comes from the engine.
- This does NOT change LEADS: J first, then a master, otherwise small; do not
  sacrifice 9 under an unseen J while a lower trump is available.
- Defence and non-trump winner/partner-feeding policies are unchanged.
  A focused journey confirms first-round side A/K uses A against an opponent's
  Q, but K to feed a partner already winning with 10. Chasing on lead and
  known-cut avoidance can also postpone a side ace. Legal availability is
  not an instruction to play every ace immediately.
- V1/v2/v3 runtime modules are byte-for-byte equal to the previously published
  versions at `f07df0d`; the transient shared clarification was restored
  before introducing v4. Default bot remains de Belote v3.
- The previously proposed defensive-cut v4 is renamed the deferred V5 idea.
  No v5 implementation, catalogue entry or benchmark is invented.
- Catalogue/factory expose v4 with David's Brooklyn family and existing names,
  no portraits. Existing three Brooklyn journey functions now cover all four
  versions, seeded console/web parity and side-ace behavior. Brooklyn,
  autoplay, claim and lobby suites pass; no frontend code changes and no
  browser verification.

## Protocol and validation

400 NEW complete games: v3 and v4 each against de Belote v2/v3, 100 per
matchup, target 1000. Seeds 30000–30049, each played on teams 0/1; dealer
`seed % 4`, bot RNG `seed * 17 + seat`. Current web engine, normal bot
settings, display sleeps/broadcasts disabled. Runtime
5.3 minutes. No policy tuning during or after the run.

All 400 unique game keys, both swaps, wins, declaring totals, success counts
and cancelled/played-deal totals reconcile to raw records. No dropped games
or outcome exclusions. Hidden hands are used only by post-choice diagnostics,
never passed to Brooklyn.

The rerun V3 control matches ALL 200 prior game outcomes and ALL its summary
metrics exactly (0 differing outcomes;
metrics match: true). This checks that
version isolation did not silently change v3. Earlier v1/v2 rows are saved
references, not relabelled as newly simulated games.

Reused development seeds are not holdout validation. Different card play
changes game lengths and later hands/contracts; paired seeds are not
identical fixed-contract replays. Comparisons are versus common opponents,
not direct Brooklyn-version head-to-head matches.
