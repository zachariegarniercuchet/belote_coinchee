# Duplicate card play — 1 November 2026

500 shared distributions; 1574 attacking suited reference conditions compared. The same hands, entire public auction, contract, bidder and opening lead are replayed with Brooklyn v1, v3 and v4 against unchanged de Belote v2/v3. These are single-deal comparisons, not full-game win rates.

V4 performs better in this frozen-contract population: +2.576 earned points
per condition versus V1 (95% interval +0.947 to +4.374), and 49 more contracts
made (85 saved, 36 lost). Success increases from 83.35% to 86.47%, a paired
+3.113 percentage points (95% interval +1.199 to +5.006). The direction is
positive under both reference-auction generators and both opponents.
V4 also improves over V3; V3's smaller improvement over V1 remains uncertain.
These results assess the revised card policies with public belote information,
not the earlier published full-game/bidding combinations.

## What differs

- V1 and V2 use the same card-play methods. V3 adds last-trump retention after a trump lead, with the partner-opener return exception. V4 shares that lead policy after the latest clarification and takes the largest winning trump when following trump in attack; V1/V3 take the smallest sufficient winning trump. Cutting remains smallest-sufficient in attack for all three.
- Actual public belote/rebelote notifications are available to every candidate and opponent, including the known unplayed mate. Hidden unannounced pairs are not passed. This common information fix precedes the frozen comparison.

## Exploratory first follow difference: V4 versus V3

These groups contain conditions whose first different card occurs at that
position when following trump. They are descriptive subgroups, not a test
of a separately implemented last-seat policy. Reference conditions can
share a distribution and are not independent.

| Position | Conditions | Unique distributions | Mean earned-point change | Saved / lost contracts |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Second | 100 | 57 | +5.800 | 14 / 3 |
| Third | 384 | 147 | +5.742 | 23 / 7 |
| Last | 37 | 25 | -0.973 | 0 / 1 |

The observed benefit mainly comes before the last player. The small last-seat
subgroup merits a separate controlled variant before changing that rule;
no policy was retuned on this sample.

## Controls and protocol

- Source `c7be4ffc262cd1db473d47e19c5c019e0e22a1d1`. Seeds 50000–50499; both teams, de Belote v2/v3, two common auction generators (Brooklyn v1/v4). Initial leader is `(dealer - 1) % 4`.
- All 230 played control conditions match in complete plays and scores: {'defense_suit': 192, 'TOUT_ATOUT': 16, 'SANS_ATOUT': 22}.
- 4,000 reference conditions are retained, including cancelled auctions and unplayed controls. 1674 distinct conditions were physically replayed with three card policies. Identical histories under the two references reuse exact seeded results and remain marked in raw data.
- Primary selection depends only on the common auction (Brooklyn attacking a suited contract), before any candidate card play. Every such condition is retained. Defense/SA/TA controls cover the first 50 seeds. No failed contract or capot is removed.
- Candidate proposed bids are ignored after the normal own-hand/public-history observation call; the whole common legal auction is forced. Normal web-engine contract/trick/belote notifications, legal cards, claims and final-trick automation remain active.
- Assertions verify identical hands/history/contract/leader and that the first divergent decision belongs to the compared team and expected trump-play branch. All bots use `Random(seed * 17 + seat)`; deck RNG is `Random(seed)`.
- The audit also checked the same initial card, exactly one remaining trump
  and a prior trump round for every first lead difference, and a stronger
  winning requested trump for every first follow difference. No retained-last-
  trump difference violated the partner-opener return exception.
- Main estimates pool the declared reference conditions. Strata show both bidding generators separately; their overlapping conditions are not independent. Confidence intervals use 4,000 percentile bootstrap draws of whole distribution seeds (RNG 20261101), including both references, opponents and team swaps, with ratio-of-totals weighting.
- Four workers; wall elapsed 1293.2s, including any session suspension. This is not CPU runtime. The running server is not contacted or restarted.
- Reproduce: `python -B benchmarks/cardplay.py --seeds 500 --seed 50000 --control-seeds 50 --workers 4 --output results.json`.
