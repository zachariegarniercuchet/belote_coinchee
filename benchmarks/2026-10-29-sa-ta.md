# Brooklyn with SA/TA — 29 October 2026

800 complete games: Brooklyn v1–v4 against de Belote v2/v3, 100 games per matchup. V1 wins 101/200, v2 80/200, v3 87/200, v4 88/200. This sample does not establish a reliable advantage of v4 over the other versions. New SA/TA declarations are markedly less reliable than suited contracts for v3/v4. No policy was tuned on these results.

This snapshot predates the subsequent user-specified Brooklyn purge policy
(three cards with the top two / four with the top one, preserving short
masters). Its results remain attached to the revision below, not to that
newer card-play behavior.

## Source and protocol

- Source revision: `dc31e65c403c4e88e3b8d8dff40198b35f51184a` (includes the completed SA/TA implementation and mode-aware reporting).
- Raw records: [results-2026-10-29-sa-ta.json](results-2026-10-29-sa-ta.json).
- Web engine; target 1000; seeds 30000–30049, each on teams 0 and 1; dealer `seed % 4`; bot RNG `seed * 17 + seat`.
- Four independent worker processes; display sleeps/broadcasts disabled. No connection to the live local/Render server; no restart, push or deployment.
- Runtime: 657.39 seconds (about 11.0 minutes).
- Brooklyn may bid all six contracts. De Belote v2/v3 retain suited bidding; when defending SA/TA they use the implemented common public-information mode policy, matching current in-app behavior. Their single-trump simulations are not used for SA/TA.
- Capots are included in game wins, taking, success and scoring. Only the explicitly labeled ordinary-margin view excludes capot deals. Numeric mean bids exclude explicit Capot bids.
- Taking excludes passed-out deals. Success uses the actual scoring-engine flag, including the majority requirement. Margin is earned trick points + dix de der + belotes minus the numeric contract; it excludes contract/coinche awards.
- Source and policies were fixed before launch. A concurrent UI-only commit does not change these bots or the engine.

Command:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B benchmarks/bots.py --brooklyn brooklyn_v1 brooklyn_v2 brooklyn_v3 brooklyn_v4 --pairs 50 --workers 4 --output benchmarks/results-2026-10-29-sa-ta.json
```

## Validation

- 800 unique `(Brooklyn, opponent, seed, team)` keys; all 8 matchups contain exactly 100 games and all 50 complete team-swapped seed pairs.
- Summaries independently recomputed from raw records match every saved row and comparison. Mode counts/success counts sum to each overall row; no games or failed contracts dropped.
- SA/TA have null single-suit trump-count/J–9 diagnostics; they are excluded only from suited-opening and trump-hunting diagnostics, never from wins, taking, success or calibration.
- Hidden hands used only after choices for diagnostics, never provided to bots.
